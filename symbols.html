Symbol review

For the specialist's eye.

The draft catalogue behind the Writing Room's Symbols panel. Nothing here has been reviewed by a Tibetologist. Mark each entry, add a note where something is wrong or missing, then copy or send the review.

What to check

  • Is the label how a scholar would name the mark?
  • Is the category sensible, and is the EWTS what you would type?
  • Is anything missing, or misleading?

Evidence says where an entry comes from: a fixture file means that EWTS to Unicode pair is listed there (other converters agree where noted in the repository), and “engine” means only T-Time’s own output shows it. Your marks stay in this page until you copy or send them.
