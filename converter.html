Diagnostics

    Exact: converting the EWTS back gives your Unicode unchanged. Canonical: equal after canonical normalization. Lossy: some characters have no EWTS spelling and appear as \uXXXX escapes. A round trip that differs is a finding worth reporting.

    Advanced

    Writes a few Tibetan letters as single precomposed characters (for example U+0F43) instead of their standard decomposed sequence. The result is not in Unicode normalization form NFC, so text compared or searched elsewhere may not match. Leave it off unless a font or program needs it.

    Report a problem

    Nothing is sent anywhere. Review the text, then copy it into an email or note.
